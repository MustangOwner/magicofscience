Magic of Science: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

The short version

Magic of Science does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your device so it can remember them next time: your chosen language, your sound setting, your favourites, which items you have opened, your quiz records, and whether the daily reminder is on. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are created locally; no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Sharing

When you share a fact, the app makes a picture and hands it to the share sheet on your device. What happens next depends on the app you choose to share with.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
