Common questions

How do I change the language? Tap the settings button at the top right of any tab and pick English, Русский, Español or Français.

How do I turn sound off? The same settings button has a "Sound and haptics" switch. Sounds are also silent when your iPhone's ring switch is off.

How do I stop the daily reminder? Turn off "Daily reminder at 9:00" in the settings button, or switch off notifications for the app in the iPhone's Settings.

Does the app need the internet? No. Everything works offline.

Is my data collected? No. See the privacy policy.
